// logo and colours

The mark: a flywheel, its crank pin and an open bore.

Every file here is generated from the same four shapes. Use the SVG wherever you can; the PNG and JPG versions are rendered from it by tools/logo/render.sh.

The logo on its own, for dark backgrounds. Accent #f0883e; transparent, with an open bore.

// palette

Dark first, one accent.

The same tokens as assets/site.css. The accent is the only colour that means "us"; green means verified, and the two data colours belong to charts.

Background#0d1117
Surface#161b22
Border#30363d
Text#e6edf3
Muted#8b949e
Accent#f0883e
Accent on light#bc4c00
OK#3fb950
Data: speed#e06c24
Data: load#3987e5
  • Leave clear space around the mark of at least the diameter of its crank pin.
  • Below 24 px, use the favicon: it drops the arm and the bore so the mark stays legible.
  • Keep the mark one colour: #f0883e on dark, #bc4c00 on light. Do not outline, stretch or rotate it.
